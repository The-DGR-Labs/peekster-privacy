PEEKSTER

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Peekster ("the App"), developed and published by DGR Labs ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use the application. We aim to keep your personal information private and give you control over your digital wellbeing data.

1. Quick Overview

Peekster has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertisements and no analytics. Everything it records about how you use your phone stays on your device and is not routinely sent to us. The exception is when you voluntarily contact us or send a diagnostic report, or when you purchase Peekster Pro, which is handled by Google Play. We cannot sell or share your usage information that we never receive.

In normal use:

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not operate our own servers to collect your app usage data.
  • Your usage statistics and app settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements.
  • We do not use third-party analytics services to track your activity.
  • Google Play handles payments if you purchase Peekster Pro.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, Peekster may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Short-video and social media usage counts.
  • Usage timestamps and time spent on supported app screens.
  • Daily and historical usage statistics.
  • App limits, focus settings, blocked-app selections and challenge settings.
  • Notification preferences and other app preferences.
  • Temporary information used to detect and count supported content.
  • Onboarding answers and configuration choices.
  • Peekster Pro subscription status and expiry information.

This information is used to operate the App's counting, statistics, focus, restriction and digital wellbeing features. It is not routinely uploaded to DGR Labs.

3. Accessibility Service

Peekster uses Android's Accessibility Service, which you must explicitly enable in your device settings, to provide its usage monitoring and restriction features.

The service may be used to:

  • Identify which supported application is currently being used.
  • Recognize supported screens or content areas for counting purposes.
  • Determine when selected app or content limits have been reached.
  • Help enforce focus sessions and restrictions selected by you.

Depending on the restriction feature you enable, Peekster may use Android accessibility actions, such as Back or Home, to help enforce those restrictions.

Peekster does not use the Accessibility Service to collect, copy or transmit your messages, passwords, payment details or private screen content to DGR Labs. Information accessed by the service is used only to provide the App's functionality.

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time through your Android device settings. Some Peekster features will stop working when it is disabled.

4. Other Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your device, Peekster may request the following permissions or special access:

  • Display over other apps: To display the floating counter or other on-screen elements.
  • Notifications: To show reminders, usage alerts and focus-related notifications.
  • Vibration: To provide optional feedback for certain interactions.
  • Run at startup: To restore supported monitoring or restriction functionality after your device restarts, where configured.
  • Usage access: Where required, to support app usage monitoring features.
  • Installed applications information: To identify applications that you may choose to monitor or restrict.
  • Battery optimization settings: To help you configure background operation where Android restricts it.
  • Device administrator access: If you enable a feature that requires it, such as an additional Strict Mode protection.

These permissions are used to provide the features you choose to use. You can manage or revoke permissions through Android settings, although doing so may affect certain features.

5. Data Backup

Some app data may be included in Android's device backup system, depending on your device settings and Android configuration. Such backups may be stored in your Google account or associated backup service.

DGR Labs does not operate or control Google's backup infrastructure and cannot access your personal Google account backups. You can manage Android backup settings through your device or Google account.

6. Peekster Pro and Google Play Billing

Peekster Pro is a paid subscription offered through Google Play.

When you subscribe, restore a purchase or open Peekster, the App asks Google Play on your device whether your subscription is active. This allows Peekster to determine whether Pro features should be available.

Google handles your payment, purchase details and subscription under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details, and Google Play does not give us your name, email address or Google account through the App's subscription process.

Peekster keeps only the subscription plan and relevant expiry information in protected storage on your device.

The Google Play Billing software built into Peekster is provided by Google. It may exchange purchase-related and technical information with Google to provide the billing service. That information is handled by Google, not by us.

Your purchases are also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

7. Support Requests and Diagnostic Information

If you contact us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and description of the issue.

If you voluntarily choose to share a diagnostic or counting report, it may contain technical information such as:

  • Peekster app version.
  • Device model and Android version.
  • Permission and accessibility status.
  • Relevant screen labels or technical information used to investigate a counting or functionality issue.

You can review and edit any report before sending it. We use information you voluntarily provide only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues and improve the App.

Peekster does not automatically send these support or diagnostic reports to DGR Labs. They are received only if you choose to send them to us.

8. Advertising and Analytics

Peekster does not display third-party advertisements and does not use third-party analytics services to track your behavior across applications or websites.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Your locally stored usage information is not routinely shared with DGR Labs or other third parties. Google may process information necessary for Google Play Billing and Android services, according to its own policies.

Information you voluntarily send to us, such as a support request or diagnostic report, will be used for the purposes described in this policy.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Usage information and settings stored by Peekster remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to any Android backup copies or other device-level retention.

You can remove locally stored App data by clearing Peekster's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Any Android backup copies may need to be managed separately through your Google account.

Information you voluntarily send to our support email may remain in our email records for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request and maintain support records.

11. Children's Privacy

Peekster is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration, as the App does not require an account.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request or other communication, please contact us so we can review the matter.

12. Security

We design Peekster to keep its core usage information on your device rather than routinely transmitting it to our servers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

We recommend keeping your device operating system updated and using appropriate device security protections.

13. Third-Party Services

Peekster may rely on Android system services and Google Play Billing to provide certain functionality. These services are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies and terms.

We encourage you to review the relevant policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Peekster's features, functionality or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
